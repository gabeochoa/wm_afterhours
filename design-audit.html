Scope and review conventions

This is an audit of committed 1280×720 captures. Static screenshots do not establish keyboard support, hover states, smooth animation, or performance. A visible defect is separated from a proposed design change. Intentional test fixtures should remain intact.

P1 marks obscured or unreadable content. P2 affects reading or comparison. P3 covers polish or added explanation. Ownership starts in wm; these findings do not establish that afterhours needs a new API.

Only existing handcrafted web mocks are shown as mocks. Screens without one still have their baseline and findings. Older captures inherit the applicable current-screen findings, with differences called out. Queue selections stay in this browser and can be exported; they do not mark code as fixed.
