Research-only. Sources are public web via `meta mase.browse` (search + open). Every non-obvious claim has a URL; everything else is marked **UNVERIFIED** rather than guessed. Game identity itself is ambiguous for 5 screens (noted per-screen) — matching the wrong title is the biggest fidelity risk.

## (2) 10 highest-value corrections for the follow-up fidelity pass

1. **PowerWash:** replace WM's 5 tabs with **General / Graphics / Washing / Sound**; move destructive **CLEAR save** into General; delete the GAMEPLAY tab. (Highest confidence, largest error.)
2. **Angry Birds:** use the **two-gears entry + vertical list** (Language, Support, Credits, Terms & Privacy, Sound, No Power-Ups, Data Collection, Community, What's New); make Support/Community external links. But first confirm the screenshot is Reloaded, not Classic/2.
3. **Ace Combat:** drop the generic 4-tab model; make **Standard vs Expert** the hero control and lock difficulty per mission; remove FOV/shake/blur rows. But first confirm which Ace Combat entry.
4. **Dead Space:** promote **Accessibility** to a full category (~25 rows incl. Menu Narration, Colorblind, Aim Assist/Magnetism, subtitle customization) and add **Graphics Preset**; don't collapse to sliders.
5. **Kirby Air Riders:** lock tabs/rows to Game8's list — 5 separate Sound channels (0–130), Online = visibility toggles (Class/Global Win Power/machine customs), Controller = Boost Charge hold/release + camera axes. Verify Delete Data before shipping it.
6. **BlazBlue Cross Tag:** model the **Entrance hub** with per-category submenus (Offline ≫ 7 modes incl. Tactics/Gallery/Replay Theater; Online ≫ Casual/Rank/Room; Customize = Item Shop with P$ items). Options rows stay UNVERIFIED until sourced.
7. **Parcel Corps:** replace "phone settings" with the **delivery phone** (nav app, text replies, QR scan); reflect the real near-zero options (subtitles only). A toggle-filled settings app is the opposite of the design.
8. **Rematch:** rename VIDEO → **Display**; add Server/HUD/Camera groups under Gameplay (Minimap Mode/Size, FOV 80, Camera Shake 0); make Controls **preset-based** (Default/Cam-Lock, vibration), not free rebinding.
9. **Rubber Bandits:** move mode-select to **The Jail lobby sign**, and treat Supporter Pack as a **store bundle** (Go Wild/Fur Party/soundtrack), not a menu row. Remove unverified OPTIONS..BACK ordering and LB/RB cycling claims.
10. **CozyCafe, NeonStrike, Dream Inc.: stop and re-identify.** Three of 14 screens cannot be verified because the games were never correctly named. Re-match the screenshots to titles (and check Game UI Database directly) before any fidelity work — building on assumed identities risks compounding errors across the other 11.
